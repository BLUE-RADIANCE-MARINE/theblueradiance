Charter & Management

One responsibility,
start to finish.

Chartering and management are a single service line at Blue Radiance. A vessel supplied on charter can be operated by us under full technical, crewing and compliance responsibility — so the client contracts once and holds one party accountable.

  • Vessels supplied to owners, operators and contractors
  • Crewed, maintained and certified to the work
  • Coordinated with construction, procurement and personnel scopes

Chartered or fully managed, the vessel stays our responsibility until the scope is complete.
