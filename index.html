Our Core Business

Our flagship service.
Everything else supports it.

Blue Radiance is a Saudi-registered marine services provider headquartered in Al Khobar. Our core business is vessel chartering and vessel management. Around that core we deliver construction, procurement, equipment and personnel — contracted as one accountable scope or as individual work packages.
