About Blue Radiance.

Vessels, crews and equipment managed to standard — so our clients' operations continue safely and on schedule.

01 — Who We Are

A marine services provider
with a core.

Blue Radiance Marine Company is a Saudi-registered marine services provider headquartered in Al Khobar. Our core business is vessel chartering and vessel management — supplying offshore support vessels to owners, operators and contractors, and operating them under full technical, crewing and compliance responsibility.

Around that core we deliver offshore construction & commissioning support, marine procurement, equipment supply, skilled offshore manpower and marine support services — contracted as one accountable scope or as individual work packages.

We operate from the Eastern Province, the centre of the Kingdom's marine and energy activity, within reach of Dammam, Ras Tanura, Jubail and the offshore fields of the Arabian Gulf. Our operations team is available around the clock.

Est. 2026 · Al Khobar

Our Direction

Vision, mission & values.

Vision

To be a dependable, professionally managed provider of chartered and managed offshore vessels in the Kingdom — setting consistent standards in operational quality, safety performance and client service, and supporting Saudi Arabia's maritime growth in line with Vision 2030.

Mission

To charter, operate and manage vessels — and to supply the crews, equipment and materials around them — safely, efficiently and transparently, with 24/7 responsiveness and disciplined reporting on every engagement.

Values

Integrity, safety and reliability guide every engagement. We operate transparently, respect our commitments, and shape responsive, client-centred solutions around each operation we support.
