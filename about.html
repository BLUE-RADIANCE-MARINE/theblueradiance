Blue Radiance Marine Company is a Saudi-registered offshore marine company built on a simple conviction: clients should feel the discipline of an operation before they ever see a vessel.

Who We Are

From the Eastern Province,
for the Gulf.

We are headquartered in Al Khobar, in the Eastern Province of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — the working coastline of the region's energy industry. Our core business is vessel chartering and vessel management; every other service we offer exists to keep those vessels compliant, supplied and on hire.

The company serves offshore operators, EPC contractors, ship owners and marine procurement teams — organisations that audit before they trust, and that measure a marine partner by uptime, paperwork and how the phone is answered at 03:00.

That is the standard we recruit to, operate to, and expect to be held to.

Offshore, Every Day

Operating Principles

Three rules we
don't negotiate.

Safety before schedule

No charter rate or deadline outranks the safety of crews, vessels and installations. Clients hire us because that ordering never changes.

Paper as strong as steel

Certification, crew credentials and HSE records are maintained inspection-ready at all times. If it isn't documented, it isn't done.

Say it plainly

Rates, terms, vessel status, problems — communicated in clear language, early. Executives should never have to decode a marine contractor.
