Supporting Disciplines

Six services.
One line of responsibility.

/ 03

Ship Agency

Port calls at Saudi ports handled end to end — inward and outward clearance, berth coordination, crew changes, provisions and husbandry. One agent, one point of accountability, from arrival notice to departure.

/ 04

Offshore Marine Operations

Marine coordination for field campaigns: vessel scheduling, cargo runs, personnel movements and offshore logistics support planned against the operator's programme, not around it.

Offshore Construction Support

Marine support for construction and installation scopes — support vessels, anchor handling, marine crews and coordination delivered under one contract structure.

Marine Procurement

Spares, consumables and project materials sourced through marine-grade supply lines, with documentation and traceability that survive client audit.
