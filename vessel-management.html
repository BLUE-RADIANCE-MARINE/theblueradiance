Scope of Management

Three disciplines,
one accountable manager.

Management at Blue Radiance covers the vessel, the people aboard it and the movement it performs — held together by one operations team.

Vessel Operations & Technical Management

Day-to-day operation, technical management and planned maintenance of the vessel.

Crew Management & Marine Compliance

Crewing, certification, and compliance with flag, class and client requirements.

Voyage, Fleet & Offshore Logistics

Voyage planning, fleet coordination, mobilisation, demobilisation and offshore logistics.

Standard of Care

Managed to standard.

A managed vessel carries an owner's asset and a charterer's programme. We treat both as our responsibility: vessels, crews and equipment managed to standard, so our clients' operations continue safely and on schedule.

  • Continuous coordination and round-the-clock oversight
  • Accurate reporting keeps owners and operators informed at every stage
  • Structured procedures and defined responsibilities
  • Performance reviewed and processes refined over time
