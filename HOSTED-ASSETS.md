# Hosted assets — do not delete

This repository is a **public web root**. Files here are live URLs on
`https://theblueradiance.com/`, and several are used by systems outside this
website. A file being unreferenced by the site's own HTML does **not** mean it
is unused.

## In active external use

| File | Used by |
|---|---|
| `images/Img.PNG` | CodeTwo email signature — company logo |
| `images/Blue_radiance_banner.png` | CodeTwo email signature — footer banner |
| `images/Blue_radiance_banner_v2.png` | Email signature / marketing variant |
| `images/blue_radiance_logo.png` | Full logo lockup, external documents |
| `sujal.html` | Digital contact card, may be on printed cards or QR codes |

These are hot-linked from email signatures sent to clients. Removing one
breaks the signature in **every email already delivered**, not just future
ones, because mail clients fetch the image at open time.

## Rule

Do not delete anything from this repository on the grounds that the website
does not link it. Confirm with the business owner first. Disk space here is
free; a broken client-facing image is not.
